    ### Effective Open Source Contributions: A Guide to Success

Contributing to open source projects (OSPs) is a valuable way 
to improve your skills, help others, and make a positive impact. 
It’s important to be clear about why you're contributing, whether 
it’s to learn or help fix issues. The key to success is being polite,
respectful, and clear in communication with other contributors. 

Stay focused on one task at a time, follow the project’s guidelines, 
and always try to solve problems on your own before asking for help.
 OSPs work democratically, but core decisions are made by a small group
 of maintainers. Always be patient and respect others’ time since many
 people contribute in their free time.
